- Publish, sell or distribute a changed version under the name CAD Master, or a name that could be mistaken for it.

A changed version must use its own name and its own icons, in the program, the installer, the add-in list and the download page.

## Official releases
Official releases come only from https://github.com/Nilove2018/cad-master-solidworks/releases. CAD Master's in-app updates install only from there, and check every download against its published checksum.

To ask for permission or report a copy that pretends to be CAD Master, open an issue or contact the author through GitHub.
